Profile

Neurologist and clinician-researcher specializing in Movement Disorders and advanced therapies, with clinical fellowship training at NYU Langone Health and a PhD background in quantitative neuroimaging.

Research interests focus on integrating advanced MRI biomarkers with clinical outcomes to improve patient selection, treatment targeting and personalization of DBS and MR-guided focused ultrasound.

Clinical & research interests

  • Movement Disorders & Parkinson’s disease: outpatient management, advanced therapies and longitudinal clinical follow-up.
  • Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS): programming and clinical management of DBS patients with Parkinson’s disease and dystonia.
  • MR-guided Focused Ultrasound (MRgFUS): patient selection, peri-procedural assessment and post-treatment follow-up.
  • Advanced neuroimaging: structural MRI, diffusion MRI, QSM/susceptibility-based imaging and imaging biomarkers.
  • Clinical research & biostatistics: longitudinal studies, mixed-effects models, data visualization and translational clinical research.
